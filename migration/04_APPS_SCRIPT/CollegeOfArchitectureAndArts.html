BRIEF / PROGRAMME

This architectural project, the College of Architecture and Arts, serves approximately 1,500 students. The project integrates educational, administrative, and social spaces within an efficient campus layout, emphasizing accessibility and inclusivity. Key facilities include lecture halls, studios, laboratories, libraries, exhibition halls, and a central auditorium to support learning and collaboration. The design balances functional needs, spatial organization, and architectural expression, promoting academic excellence and social interaction.
