CELLPHONE STORE

SYRIA, HAMA

- 2026 -

OVERVIEW

This 13 m² cellphone store was renovated and reconfigured from a former thrift shop in poor condition into a modern retail space. The project involved extensive repairs and a comprehensive redesign to accommodate the functional requirements of a cellphone store within a highly limited area. The design prioritized space optimization, functionality, and operational efficiency, with careful consideration of display integration, circulation, customer capacity, and staff convenience. The result is a compact, contemporary retail environment that maximizes the potential of every square meter without compromising usability or visual appeal.

WORK DEVELOPMENT STAGES

1-Inspecting

The existing electrical and plumbing systems were assessed to determine what could be retained or required modification. Site measurements were taken to evaluate the layout and identify opportunities to improve space utilization and functionality. A key decision was to replace the existing spiral staircase, which occupied approximately 13% of the store’s floor area, with a more compact alternative to maximize usable retail space.

2-Planning and Desgining

Detailed designs, 3D cabinet modeling and visualization, technical drawings, and shop drawings were developed for the store’s custom cabinetry, including drawer and door mechanisms. Every element was carefully planned to maximize space utilization and functionality. The design was refined through detailed client consultations, ensuring all requirements were addressed before the final proposal received formal approval.

3-Site Preperation

The space was cleared of existing contents, fixtures, and the old shopfront and glazing to prepare for renovation. The upper floor was designated for storage, with an additional WC incorporated into the layout at the client's request, requiring further plumbing provisions and modifications.

4-Electricty

Following the detailed planning phase, gypsum board ceiling installation, lighting layouts, and electrical outlets were implemented according to the approved CAD drawings. Accurate site measurements ensured precise coordination between the design and on-site execution, minimizing errors and maintaining alignment with the planned layout.

5-Flooring

Following the completion of electrical and plumbing works, flooring works commenced. Materials were carefully selected to complement the store’s modern design while ensuring durability, reliability, and suitability for daily commercial use.

6-Cabinetry Works

The store began to take shape as custom cabinetry work progressed. Each stage was carefully inspected, with minor on-site adjustments made to accommodate actual site conditions while maintaining the original design intent. Materials and finishes were selected to achieve the intended aesthetic while ensuring functionality and durability.

7-Finalizing

The final stage focused on installing the remaining elements, including tables and shelving, while addressing outstanding cabinetry adjustments and on-site issues. With the final details completed, the store came together as a cohesive, modern retail space, maximizing the potential of its 13 m² area through thoughtful design, efficient planning, and careful attention to functionality.
