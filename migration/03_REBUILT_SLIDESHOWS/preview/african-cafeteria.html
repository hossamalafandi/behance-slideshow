The Concept

UNIVERSITY CAFETERIA

A social and cultural hub.

The cafeteria space is conceived not only as a place for dining but as a social and cultural hub.

THE PROPOSAL

The project is an interior design proposal for a university cafeteria, developed within the framework of exploring diverse decorative styles. The chosen concept centers on the African aesthetic, aiming to create a vibrant and authentic spatial experience that reflects cultural identity while maintaining functionality and comfort.

MATERIALITY

The design approach was grounded in research into the materials and techniques most common in African environments. Natural elements such as clay, wood, brick, straw, and African plants were employed to emphasize warmth and authenticity. In addition, traditional African accessories and handcrafted details were incorporated to narrate the lifestyle and heritage of the continent, enriching the atmosphere with cultural depth.

ATMOSPHERE

The cafeteria space is conceived not only as a place for dining but as a social and cultural hub, where the design addresses the need for an engaging and immersive environment. The spatial composition, material palette, and decorative details come together to transform the cafeteria into a welcoming setting that harmonizes functionality with a distinctive aesthetic identity.
